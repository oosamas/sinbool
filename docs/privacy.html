Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Sinbool ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of children who use our app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information in the Sinbool app.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Sinbool is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide the app experience:

  • Usage Data: Lessons completed, reading progress, and streak information stored locally on your device.
  • Device Information: Anonymous crash reports to help us fix bugs and improve the app (via Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Language Preferences: Your selected language setting.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Names, email addresses, or other personal identifying information from children
  • Location data
  • Photos, videos, or audio
  • Contact lists or social media information

How We Use Information

  • To track learning progress within the app
  • To improve app stability through anonymous crash reporting
  • To provide the correct language experience

Data Storage

All learning progress and preferences are stored locally on your device by default. If you choose to create an account and enable cloud sync, data is stored securely using Firebase services with encryption in transit and at rest.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, configured to comply with COPPA:

  • Firebase Analytics: Anonymized usage analytics with no personal data collection.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Anonymous crash reporting to improve app stability.

Parental Controls

Sinbool includes PIN-protected parental controls that allow parents to:

  • Manage app settings
  • Access and delete their child's data at any time
  • Control cloud sync preferences

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time by:

  • Using the "Delete Data" option in Settings (under Parental Controls)
  • Uninstalling the app from your device
  • Contacting us at privacy@sinbool.com

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date above.
